VaraVaults

Player vaults for your Minecraft server

Download

About

Give your players safe, good-looking vaults to store anything they want. Pink & white colors with small caps text, fully editable in the config.

Features

  • Vault selector with pages, locked vaults and live slot counts
  • Custom icons — Shift + Click a vault to pick its icon
  • H2 & SQLite storage, light and lag-free
  • LuckPerms support — new vaults show up instantly
  • Dupe-safe — owner and admin share the same live vault
  • PlugManX-safe — open vaults are saved before /plugman reload, load, unload & /reload
  • Every text, color, icon and sound is editable

Player commands

  • /pv /vault /varavault Open the vault selector
  • /pv <number> Open a vault directly

Admin commands

  • /pvadmin Admin help (same as /varavaultsadmin)
  • /pvadmin reload Reload the config
  • /pvadmin forceopen <player> Open the selector for a player
  • /pvadmin view <player> <number> View any player's vault (even offline)

Permissions

  • varavaults.vault.<number> Access to that one vault (vault #1 is free by default)
  • varavaults.selector Open the selector — default: everyone
  • varavaults.openremote Use /pv <number> — default: everyone
  • varavaults.itempicker Change vault icons — default: everyone
  • varavaults.admin Admin commands — default: OP
  • varavaults.admin.edit Edit (not just view) other players' vaults

Requirements

  • Paper or Purpur 1.21.x (Java 21)
  • LuckPerms installed — download it on Modrinth

About

A lightweight but feature-rich trade plugin. Both players put in items and money, both click Ready, and after a short countdown everything is swapped safely. Pink & white colors with small caps text, fully editable in the config.

Features

  • All items supported — even items with custom names, enchants and NBT
  • Multi-currency — Experience (built in), Vault money, PlayerPoints
  • Custom currencies with PlaceholderAPI — works with any economy plugin
  • Start trades easily — Shift + right-click a player, or use /trade
  • Clickable [ACCEPT] / [DENY] buttons in chat
  • Shulker & barrel viewer — right-click to look inside before you accept
  • Trade summaries — see exactly what you gave and got
  • Trade history — every trade is saved to daily log files
  • Blacklist — block items, worlds and gamemodes
  • Number formatting — type 1.5K or 10M, shown as compact or full numbers
  • Item names — nice names in summaries, with your own translations
  • Dupe-safe — offered items and money are held safely until the trade ends
  • Folia supported, very light, doesn't affect TPS
  • Any client version (ViaVersion) and Bedrock (Geyser) players can trade

Commands

  • /trade <player> Send a trade request
  • /trade accept <player> Accept a trade request
  • /trade deny <player> Deny a trade request
  • /trade toggle Turn trade requests on/off
  • /trade help List commands
  • /trade reload Reload the config (admin)
  • /trade force <player> [player2] Force a trade (admin)
  • /trade preview Open the trade menu for testing (admin)

Also works as /varatrade and /vtrade.

Permissions

  • varatrade.trade Send / accept / deny requests — default: everyone
  • varatrade.toggle /trade toggle — default: everyone
  • varatrade.shiftclick Shift + right-click to trade — default: everyone
  • varatrade.admin reload, force, preview — default: OP
  • varatrade.bypass.blacklist Ignore the blacklist — default: OP

Placeholders

  • %varatrade_active_trades% How many trades are going on
  • %varatrade_enabled% Does the player accept trade requests?
  • %varatrade_partner% The player's trade partner (empty if not trading)
  • %varatrade_trading% Is the player in a trade? true / false

Requirements

  • Spigot, Paper, Purpur or Folia 1.20.6 - 1.21.x (Java 21)
  • No required plugins
