About

Give your players safe, good-looking vaults to store anything they want. Pink & white colors with small caps text, fully editable in the config.

Features

  • Vault selector with pages, locked vaults and live slot counts
  • Custom icons — Shift + Click a vault to pick its icon
  • H2 & SQLite storage, light and lag-free
  • LuckPerms support — new vaults show up instantly
  • Dupe-safe — owner and admin share the same live vault
  • PlugManX-safe — open vaults are saved before /plugman reload, load, unload & /reload
  • Every text, color, icon and sound is editable

Player commands

  • /pv /vault /varavault Open the vault selector
  • /pv <number> Open a vault directly

Admin commands

  • /pvadmin Admin help (same as /varavaultsadmin)
  • /pvadmin reload Reload the config
  • /pvadmin forceopen <player> Open the selector for a player
  • /pvadmin view <player> <number> View any player's vault (even offline)

Permissions

  • varavaults.vault.<number> Access to that one vault (vault #1 is free by default)
  • varavaults.selector Open the selector — default: everyone
  • varavaults.openremote Use /pv <number> — default: everyone
  • varavaults.itempicker Change vault icons — default: everyone
  • varavaults.admin Admin commands — default: OP
  • varavaults.admin.edit Edit (not just view) other players' vaults

Requirements

  • Paper or Purpur 1.21.x (Java 21)
  • LuckPerms installed — download it on Modrinth
